# Project 01 — Decision Trees / Random Forests

Your task is to understand the inner-working of **decision trees**, and in a bigger scale, **random forests**. Then, use random forest to predict solar flares. The challenges in the applied portion of the project should guide you to expand on the theoretical portion of the project.

## Resources (scikit-learn)

**Call the model**

- [`DecisionTreeClassifier`](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.tree.DecisionTreeClassifier.html) — `sklearn.tree`
